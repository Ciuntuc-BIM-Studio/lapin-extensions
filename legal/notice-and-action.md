# Lapin Extensions: reporting content (notice and action)

**DRAFT — to be reviewed by a lawyer before publication.** Version 0.1, 12 November 2026.

Lapin Extensions is a hosting service in the sense of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065, the Digital Services Act (DSA). It is run by **Ciuntuc BIM Studio SRL**, Huși, Romania, CUI RO51725860, J2025031471009.

## 1. Single point of contact

For users, authors and authorities (DSA Art. 11 and 12): [hello@lapinbim.com](mailto:hello@lapinbim.com), in English or Romanian.

## 2. How to report an extension

Write to [hello@lapinbim.com](mailto:hello@lapinbim.com) (subject: "Notice: <extension id>"), or open an issue in the catalogue's repository on GitHub. A notice should give (DSA Art. 16):

1. why you consider the content illegal or against the catalogue's rules, as precisely as you can;
2. the extension's address (its page on store.lapinbim.com, or its id);
3. your name and e-mail address (not needed for a notice about child sexual abuse material);
4. a statement that you believe, in good faith, that the notice is accurate and complete.

For a copyright claim, also say which work of yours is affected and how you hold the rights to it.

## 3. What we do

- We confirm receipt without undue delay.
- We assess the notice diligently and objectively. Where the content appears clearly illegal or harmful (malware, for example), we take it out of the catalogue at once, provisionally, while we finish the assessment.
- We tell the author what was reported and why we acted (a statement of reasons, DSA Art. 17), and give them the chance to answer.
- We tell the person who sent the notice what we decided.
- Our aim is a decision within 14 days; faster for malware or clear infringements.

What a removal means: the extension is taken out of the index and its copy stops being served. Lapin no longer offers it; copies already installed stay on users' computers (once the catalogue publishes its list of revoked packages, Lapin marks them).

## 4. If you disagree

An author or a person who sent a notice may ask us to look again at a decision (write to the same address, within six months). We answer with a reasoned decision. You may also use an out-of-court dispute settlement body certified under DSA Art. 21, or go to court.

## 5. Misuse

We may stop handling notices from someone who frequently sends manifestly unfounded ones, and suspend authors who frequently publish manifestly illegal content, after a warning (DSA Art. 23).

## 6. Authorities

Orders from judicial or administrative authorities (DSA Art. 9 and 10) are handled through the same address; we confirm receipt and inform the authority of the action taken.
