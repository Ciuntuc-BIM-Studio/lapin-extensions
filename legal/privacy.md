## 2. What we process, why, and on what basis

| What | Why | Legal basis | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Your forum identity**, received from forum.lapinbim.com when you sign in to the store (DiscourseConnect): your forum user id, user name and display name. Your e-mail address is received in the sign-in message but not kept. | To let you publish and manage your extensions, and to show your name as their author. | The contract (the author terms), Art. 6(1)(b). | As long as you have an extension or a submission in the store; then deleted within 30 days of your request, except what stays in the catalogue's public history (below). |
| **A sign-in cookie** (`lapin_store`), random, holding no data about you. | To keep you signed in. | Necessary for the service you asked for. | 14 days, or until you sign out. |
| **Your submissions**: the manifest fields, the file and screenshots you upload, and the pull request made from them in the public GitHub repository `Ciuntuc-BIM-Studio/lapin-extensions`. | To review and publish your extension. | The contract, Art. 6(1)(b). | A submission that was not published: deleted 90 days after it was declined or withdrawn. A published extension: as long as it is listed; its manifest stays in the repository's public history. |
| **Download counts**: per extension and version, and per month. No IP address, no identifier, nothing about who downloaded. | To show authors and users how often an extension is used. | Not personal data. | Kept. |
| **Technical data** while a request is handled: your IP address (as Cloudflare reports it), held in memory only to apply rate limits, never written to disk; a salted hash of it for one hour at most. | To protect the service from abuse. | Legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f). | One hour, in memory. |

We do not sell data, do not use it for advertising, and do no profiling. Browsing and installing extensions needs no account.

## 3. Who receives data

- **Cloudflare, Inc.** (the network in front of our servers, DNS and the connection to them): processes requests on our behalf; transfers outside the EU are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.
- **Hetzner Online GmbH**, Germany: hosts our servers.
- **GitHub, Inc.**: the catalogue's public repository, where a submission becomes a pull request made by our account; what you put in the manifest (your author name, links) becomes public there.
- **The forum** (forum.lapinbim.com, run by us on Discourse) is the identity provider; its own [privacy policy](https://forum.lapinbim.com/privacy) applies to your forum account.
